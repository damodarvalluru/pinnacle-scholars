JEE & GATE Marking Schemes Academy + Official

Each programme is listed twice — the pattern actually used for institute papers, and the scheme published by NTA or the GATE Committee. The official bulletin always prevails.

Why two schemes? Institute monthly tests follow the academy's own pattern so that results stay comparable across the year. They are modelled on the real papers but are not the official NTA or GATE paper, and the two are never merged on this page.
