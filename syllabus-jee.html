Physics

Class XI units 1–10 and Class XII units 11–19 · 100 marks

19 Units
  • Units of measurement, SI units, fundamental and derived units
  • Least count, significant figures, errors in measurement
  • Dimensions of physical quantities, dimensional analysis and its applications
  • Measurement of length, mass, time, temperature, angle and current
  • Motion in a straight line, average and instantaneous velocity and acceleration
  • Motion in a plane, projectile motion, relative motion in one and two dimensions
  • Graphical analysis of motion, elementary equations of kinematics
  • Newton's laws, force, momentum, inertia, impulse and momentum conservation
  • Friction, static and kinetic friction, rolling friction
  • Circular motion, banking of roads, dynamics of circular motion
  • Work–energy theorem, kinetic and potential energy, conservative and non-conservative forces
  • Law of conservation of energy, power, collisions, elastic and inelastic collisions
  • Centre of mass and linear momentum, centre of mass of a system of particles
  • Moment of inertia, torque, angular momentum, relation between linear and angular quantities
  • Rotational kinetic energy, rolling without slipping, rolling with slipping
  • Angular momentum conservation, rotation about a fixed axis
  • Universal law of gravitation, gravitational field and potential, escape velocity
  • Variation of g with altitude, depth and latitude, geostationary and polar satellites
  • Kepler's laws of planetary motion, time period of satellite motion
  • Elasticity, stress–strain relations, Hooke's law, elastic potential energy
  • Pressure in fluids, Bernoulli's principle, viscosity, streamline and turbulent flow
  • Surface tension, capillarity, Stokes' law, terminal velocity
  • Thermal equilibrium, zeroth and first laws of thermodynamics, internal energy, enthalpy
  • Heat capacity, specific heat capacity, thermodynamic state variables, thermodynamic processes
  • Second law, heat engines, refrigerators, Carnot cycle, adiabatic and isothermal processes
  • Equation of state, kinetic theory of gases, mean speed, RMS speed, most probable speed
  • Degrees of freedom, equipartition of energy, mean translational kinetic energy
  • Specific heat capacity of gases, adiabatic and isobaric processes
  • Periodic motion, simple harmonic motion, energy in SHM, damped and forced oscillations
  • Traveling waves, wave equation, superposition, standing waves, resonance
  • Sound waves, speed of sound, organ pipe, beats, Doppler effect
  • Coulomb's law, electric field, field due to continuous charge distribution
  • Electric dipole, dipole field, torque on a dipole in a uniform field
  • Electric flux, Gauss's theorem and its applications, electrostatic potential and potential difference
  • Equipotential surfaces, energy of a system of charges, capacitors, dielectric, energy stored
  • Electric current, drift of electrons, Ohm's law, resistivity and conductivity
  • V-I characteristics, series and parallel combinations, temperature dependence of resistance
  • EMF, internal resistance, Kirchhoff's rules, Wheatstone bridge
  • Potentiometer, meter bridge, galvanometer
  • Magnetic field, Biot–Savart law, Ampere's circuital law, solenoids, toroids
  • Magnetic force on current-carrying conductors, moving coil galvanometer
  • Magnetisation, magnetic materials, diamagnetic, paramagnetic and ferromagnetic substances
  • Magnetic flux, Faraday's laws of induction, Lenz's law, motional EMF
  • Self induction, mutual induction, inductance, energy stored in a magnetic field
  • Eddy currents, alternating current, LCR circuits, resonance, Q-factor, power factor
  • Transformers, electric motors, induction motors
  • Displacement current, electromagnetic waves, transverse character
  • Electromagnetic spectrum, propagation in different media, applications
  • Reflection, spherical and plane mirrors, refraction, total internal reflection
  • Refractive index, lenses, optical instruments, power of a lens
  • Microscope, telescope, astronomical and terrestrial instruments
  • Wave optics: Huygens' principle, interference, Young's double slit, diffraction, polarisation
  • Electromagnetic radiation, photoelectric effect, Einstein's photoelectric equation
  • Particle nature of light: photon, de Broglie wavelength, Davisson–Germer experiment
  • Atomic models, Rutherford model, Bohr model, hydrogen atom spectrum, energy levels
  • Mass–energy relation, binding energy, nuclear fission and fusion
  • Nuclear reactor, radioactivity, decay modes, radioactive decay law
  • Energy bands in solids, conductors, insulators, semiconductors, intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors
  • P-N junction diode, Zener diode, LED, photodiode, solar cell, transistor
  • Logic gates and basic logic circuits
🧪

Chemistry

Physical · Inorganic · Organic chapters · 100 marks

16 Chapters
  • Mole concept, stoichiometry, empirical and molecular formulae
  • Concentration terms: molarity, molality, mole fraction, normality
  • Significant figures, errors in measurement, law of dilution
  • Bohr's model, quantum numbers, shapes of orbitals, electronic configuration
  • Photoelectric effect, de Broglie relation, wave nature of the electron
  • Periodic trends: atomic radius, ionisation enthalpy, electron gain enthalpy, electronegativity
  • Ionic, covalent and metallic bonding, valence bond theory, hybridisation
  • Molecular orbital theory, bond order, hydrogen bonding, dipole moment
  • VSEPR theory, geometry of molecules, structural representation, isomers
  • Gas laws, ideal gas equation, kinetic theory of gases, van der Waals equation
  • Vapour pressure, boiling point, liquid surface tension, hexagonal close packing
  • Enthalpy, entropy, Gibbs free energy, spontaneous processes
  • Hess's law, thermochemical equations, heat capacity, Kirchhoff's law
  • Calculation of entropy and Gibbs energy change for a reaction
  • Chemical and ionic equilibrium, equilibrium constant, Le Chatelier's principle
  • Calculation of pH, buffer solutions, solubility product, common ion effect
  • Distribution constants, adsorption
  • Oxidation number, balancing redox reactions by ion and oxidation number methods
  • Galvanic cells, electrode potential, Nernst equation, conductance and Kohlrausch law
  • Electrolysis, primary and secondary cells, fuel cells, accumulators
  • Rate of reaction, order and molecularity, integrated rate equations, half life
  • Arrhenius equation, effect of temperature and catalyst, Arrhenius equation problems
  • Colloids, micelles, surfactants, adsorption isotherms, catalysis
  • Group 13 and 14 elements: boron, carbon, silicon, germanium and their compounds
  • Group 15, 16, 17 and 18 elements: nitrogen, oxygen, sulphur, halogens, noble gases
  • Allotropes, oxides, oxoacids, halides and important industrial compounds
  • Transition elements, general properties, lanthanoids and lanthanoid contraction, actinoids
  • Coordination compounds, nomenclature, isomerism, Werner theory, valence bond and CFT theories
  • Metal carbonyls, bonding in organometallics, important applications
  • IUPAC nomenclature, structural isomerism, stereoisomerism, conformations
  • Electronic effects, inductive, resonance and hyperconjugation
  • Reaction mechanisms: nucleophilic and electrophilic substitution, addition and elimination, SN1/SN2, E1/E2
  • Alkanes, alkenes, alkynes: preparation, properties and reactions
  • Aromatic hydrocarbons, benzene, electrophilic substitution, side-chain oxidation
  • Haloalkanes and haloarenes: SN1 and SN2 mechanisms, optical activity, polyhalogen compounds
  • Preparation, properties and reactions of alcohols, phenols and ethers
  • Acidity of phenols, electrophilic substitution, oxidation, Lucas test
  • Preparation and reactions of aldehydes and ketones, nucleophilic addition, oxidation and reduction
  • Named reactions: aldol, Cannizzaro, Clemmensen, Wolff–Kishner, haloform, Tollens and Fehling tests
  • Carboxylic acids: preparation, acidity, Hell–Volhard–Zelinsky reaction, decarboxylation
  • Classification, basicity, preparation and reactions of amines, diazonium salts and their conversions
  • Carbohydrates, amino acids, proteins, vitamins, nucleic acids, enzymes, hormones
  • Classification of polymers, addition and condensation polymerisation, natural and synthetic rubbers
  • Drugs, poisons, agrochemicals, food preservatives and detergents, cleansers
  • Metallurgy: concentration, extraction, refining, thermodynamic principles

Mathematics

Sets · Algebra · Calculus · Geometry · Vectors · Probability · 100 marks

14 Units
  • Sets, subsets, Venn diagrams, types of relations, equivalence relations
  • Domain, range, composite, one-one, onto and invertible functions
  • Algebra of complex numbers, modulus and argument, Argand plane, De Moivre's theorem
  • Roots of unity, geometric series, representation on the Argand plane
  • Quadratic equations, nature of roots, discriminant, algebraic conditions for real roots
  • Types of matrices, operations, transpose, symmetric and skew-symmetric matrices
  • Elementary operations, inverse by two methods
  • Determinants, area of a triangle, consistency and solution of linear equations
  • Fundamental principle of counting, permutations, combinations, circular permutations
  • Binomial theorem, general and middle term, properties of binomial coefficients
  • Arithmetic, geometric and harmonic progressions, arithmetic–geometric means
  • Special sums, sum to n terms, method of differences, general terms of sequences
  • Radian measure, trigonometric functions, transformations, graph generation
  • Trigonometric identities, compound and multiple-angle formulae, sign of trigonometric functions
  • Trigonometric equations, general and principal solutions, properties of triangles
  • Slope–intercept, two-point, intercept and normal forms; angle between two lines
  • Distance of a point from a line, family of lines, homogeneous equation of a line
  • Equation of a circle in various forms, tangent to a circle, family of circles
  • Parabola: standard equations, focal and directrix properties, tangent and normal
  • Ellipse and hyperbola: equations, eccentricity, focus, directrix, latus rectum
  • Rectangular hyperbola, conic sections in polar coordinates
  • Intuitive idea of limits, algebra, squeeze and l'Hospital theorems, indeterminate forms
  • Continuity, maxima and minima, derivative as a rate measure, chain rule, implicit and logarithmic differentiation
  • Parametric forms, derivative of inverse trigonometric functions, application to rate of change
  • Rate of change of quantities, increasing / decreasing functions, tangents and normals
  • Maxima and minima, first and second derivative tests, area under curves
  • Integration as the inverse of differentiation, indefinite integrals, substitution method
  • Integration by partial fractions, trigonometric integrals, rational functions
  • Definite integrals and their properties, area under simple curves, application to economics
  • Order and degree, general and particular solutions
  • Variable separable, homogeneous differential equations, linear differential equations, exact equations
  • Scalar and vector product, dot product, projection, component, vector equations
  • Direction cosines and sines, equations of a line and a plane in space, distance of a point from a plane, shortest distance between two lines
  • Conditional probability, multiplication theorem, independence, Bayes' theorem
  • Random variables, mean and variance, binomial distribution, and the statistics of grouped data

Paper 2 — B.Arch (2A) & B.Plan (2B)

Mathematics plus a second paper, in two variants.

2 Papers
Paper 2 structure
Paper Section A Section B Marks
2A — B.Arch Mathematics — 100 marks, common set with Paper 1 Aptitude Test (200) + Drawing / Perception & Design Ability Test (100) 400
2B — B.Plan Mathematics — 100 marks, common set with Paper 1 Aptitude Test (200) + Planning-based questions (100) 400

The Aptitude Test covers logical reasoning, spatial ability, drawing comprehension and simple analytical reasoning. The B.Plan variant replaces the Drawing section with planning-focused aptitude questions. Pinnacle Scholars Academy runs dedicated Paper 2 aptitude batches for both variants.
