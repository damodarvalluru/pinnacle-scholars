A candidate may appear in one or in up to two papers. Every question tests one of five abilities — recall, comprehension, application, analysis and synthesis. Papers are marked through a normalised score, so raw marks are not directly comparable across papers. In AR and GE the optional part is selected during the exam; in GG and XH the optional part has to be chosen at the time of application; in XE and XL two optional sections are selected during the exam.

General Aptitude (GA) — Common to All Papers

15 marks in every GATE paper · 4 sub-areas

15 Marks
  • Basic English grammar: tenses, articles, adjectives, prepositions, conjunctions, verb–noun agreement and other parts of speech
  • Basic vocabulary: words, idioms and phrases in context, synonyms and antonyms
  • Reading and comprehension, narrative sequencing
  • Data interpretation: bar graphs, pie charts and other data graphs, 2- and 3-dimensional plots, maps and tables
  • Numerical computation and estimation: ratios, percentages, powers, exponents and logarithms, permutations and combinations, series
  • Mensuration and geometry, elementary statistics and probability
  • Logic: deduction and induction, analogy
  • Numerical relations and reasoning, statement–conclusion problems
  • Transformation of shapes: translation, rotation, scaling, mirroring, assembling and grouping
  • Paper folding, cutting and patterns in two and three dimensions, mental rotation and visualisation

Engineering Mathematics

13 marks in most engineering papers · 15 marks as compulsory Section A of XE

13 Marks
  • Matrices and determinants, matrix algebra, solutions of linear equations
  • Cayley–Hamilton theorem, LU decomposition, matrix inversion
  • Eigenvalues and eigenvectors, linear and orthogonal transformations
  • Limits and functions of a single variable, continuity and differentiability, indeterminate forms
  • Mean value theorems, evaluation of definite and improper integrals, theorems of integral calculus
  • Taylor series in one and two variables, maxima and minima
  • Double and triple integrals, applications of the divergence, curl and vector identities
  • First-order linear and non-linear equations, separation of variables, Euler–Cauchy's equation
  • Higher-order linear differential equations, Laplace transforms
  • Partial differential equations, initial and boundary value problems
  • Solutions of heat, wave and Laplace equations
  • Classification of second-order linear partial differential equations
  • Method of separation of variables, one-dimensional heat equation, two-dimensional Laplace equation
  • Complex numbers and polar form, analytic functions, Cauchy–Riemann equations
  • Taylor and Laurent series, Cauchy's integral theorem and integral formula, residue theorem
  • Sampling theorems, conditional probability and Bayes' theorem
  • Mean, median, mode and random variables, binomial, Poisson and normal distributions
  • Discrete and continuous distributions, linear regression and correlation analysis
  • Matrix inversion, integration by trapezoidal and Simpson's rules
  • Numerical solutions of linear and non-linear algebraic equations, Newton–Raphson method
  • Single-step and multi-step methods for differential equations, explicit Euler's method

Computer Science & Information Technology (CS) · Data Science & AI (DA)

Core paper for CS and DA · 72–85 marks

10 Units
  • Boolean algebra, combinational and sequential circuits, minimisation
  • Number representations and computer arithmetic — fixed and floating point
  • Machine instructions and addressing modes, ALU, data path and control unit
  • Instruction pipelining and pipeline hazards
  • Memory hierarchy: cache, main memory and secondary storage
  • I/O interface — interrupt-driven and DMA modes
  • Programming in C, recursion, arrays, stacks, queues, linked lists
  • Trees, binary search trees, binary heaps, graphs
  • Searching, sorting, hashing, asymptotic worst-case time and space complexity
  • Algorithm design techniques: greedy, dynamic programming, divide and conquer
  • Graph traversals, minimum spanning trees, shortest paths
  • Regular expressions and finite automata, context-free grammars and push-down automata
  • Regular and context-free languages, pumping lemma
  • Turing machines and undecidability
  • Lexical analysis, parsing, syntax-directed translation, runtime environments
  • Intermediate code generation, local optimisation
  • Data flow analyses: constant propagation, liveness analysis, common subexpression elimination
  • System calls, processes, threads, inter-process communication
  • Concurrency and synchronisation, deadlock detection and avoidance
  • CPU and I/O scheduling, memory management and virtual memory, file systems
  • ER model, relational model, relational algebra, tuple calculus, SQL
  • Integrity constraints and normal forms
  • File organisation, indexing (B and B+ trees), transactions and concurrency control
  • Layering concept: OSI and TCP/IP stacks, packet, circuit and virtual circuit switching
  • Data link layer: framing, error detection, MAC, Ethernet bridging
  • Routing protocols — shortest path, flooding, distance vector, link state; fragmentation and IP addressing, IPv4, CIDR, ARP, DHCP, ICMP, NAT
  • Transport layer: flow control, congestion control, UDP, TCP, sockets; application layer: DNS, SMTP, HTTP, FTP, email
  • Propositional and first-order logic, sets, relations, functions, partial orders, lattices
  • Monoids and groups, graphs: connectivity, matching and colouring
  • Recurrence relations, counting principles, linear programming basics

Electronics & Communication Engineering (EC)

Core paper · 72 marks with Engineering Mathematics

7 Units
  • Circuit analysis: node and mesh analysis, superposition, Thevenin, Norton, reciprocity, sinusoidal steady state, two-port parameters, Laplace solution of network equations
  • Continuous-time signals: Fourier series and transform, sampling theorem and applications
  • Discrete-time signals: DTFT, DFT, z-transform, processing of continuous-time signals
  • LTI systems: causality, stability, impulse response, convolution, poles and zeroes, frequency, group and phase delay
  • Energy bands in intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors, equilibrium carrier concentration, direct and indirect band gap
  • Carrier transport: diffusion and drift current, mobility, resistivity, generation and recombination, Poisson and continuity equations
  • P-N junction, Zener diode, BJT, MOS capacitor, MOSFET, LED, photodiode, solar cell
  • Diode circuits: clipping, clamping and rectifiers
  • BJT and MOSFET amplifiers: biasing, ac coupling, small-signal analysis, frequency response, current mirrors, differential amplifiers
  • Op-amp circuits: amplifiers, summers, differentiators, integrators, active filters, Schmitt triggers, oscillators
  • Number representations, Boolean algebra, Karnaugh map minimisation, logic gates and static CMOS implementations, arithmetic circuits, multiplexers and decoders
  • Sequential circuits: latches, flip-flops, counters, shift registers, finite state machines, propagation delay, setup and hold time
  • Data converters: sample and hold, ADCs, DACs; semiconductor memories: ROM, SRAM, DRAM; pipelining
  • Basic components, feedback principle, transfer function, block diagrams and signal flow graphs
  • Transient and steady-state analysis of LTI systems, frequency response
  • Routh–Hurwitz and Nyquist stability criteria, Bode and root-locus plots, lag, lead and lag–lead compensation
  • State variable model and solution of state equations
  • Random processes: autocorrelation, power spectral density, white noise, filtering of random signals
  • Analog communications: AM/FM modulation and demodulation, spectra, superheterodyne receivers
  • Information theory: entropy, mutual information, channel capacity theorem
  • Digital communications: PCM, DPCM, ASK/PSK/FSK/QAM, bandwidth, ISI, MAP and ML detection, matched filter, SNR and BER, Hamming codes and CRC
  • Maxwell's equations in differential and integral form, boundary conditions, wave equation, Poynting vector
  • Plane waves: reflection, refraction, polarisation, phase and group velocity, skin depth
  • Transmission lines: characteristic impedance, matching, S-parameters, Smith chart
  • Rectangular and circular waveguides, optical fibres, antennas and antenna arrays

Electrical Engineering (EE)

Core paper · 72 marks with Engineering Mathematics

7 Units
  • Network elements, KCL and KVL, node and mesh analysis, Thevenin, Norton, superposition, maximum power transfer
  • Transient response of dc and ac networks, resonance, two-port networks, balanced three-phase circuits, complex power and power factor
  • Coulomb's law, electric field intensity and flux density, Gauss's law, divergence, dielectric media, capacitance
  • Biot–Savart law, Ampere's law, curl, Faraday's law, Lorentz force, magnetic circuits, self and mutual inductance
  • Representation of continuous and discrete-time signals, shifting and scaling, LTI and causal systems
  • Fourier series for periodic signals, sampling theorem, Laplace and Z transforms, RMS and average values
  • Single-phase transformer: equivalent circuit, phasor diagram, OC and SC tests, regulation and efficiency; three-phase transformers, auto-transformers
  • Electromechanical energy conversion, DC machines: separately excited, series and shunt, characteristics and speed control
  • Three-phase induction machines: principle, types, torque-speed characteristics, equivalent circuit, starting and speed control
  • Synchronous machines: cylindrical and salient pole, regulation, parallel operation, losses and efficiency
  • Generation and transmission basics, models and performance of transmission lines and cables, economic load dispatch
  • Series and shunt compensation, insulators, distribution systems, per-unit quantities, bus admittance matrix
  • Gauss–Seidel and Newton–Raphson load flow, voltage and frequency control, power factor correction
  • Symmetrical components, fault analysis, overcurrent, differential, directional and distance protection, circuit breakers, stability
  • Mathematical modelling, feedback principle, transfer functions, block diagrams and signal flow graphs
  • Transient and steady-state analysis, Routh–Hurwitz and Nyquist criteria, Bode plots, root loci, lead and lag compensation
  • P, PI and PID controllers, state-space model, solution of state equations
  • Bridges and potentiometers, measurement of voltage, current, power, energy and power factor
  • Instrument transformers, digital voltmeters and multimeters, phase, time and frequency measurement, oscilloscopes, error analysis
  • Diode circuits, amplifiers, oscillators, operational amplifiers, active filters, timers
  • Combinational and sequential logic circuits, multiplexers and demultiplexers, Schmitt triggers, A/D and D/A converters
  • Thyristor, MOSFET and IGBT characteristics, DC–DC converters (buck, boost, buck–boost), rectifiers and inverters, PWM

Civil Engineering (CE) & Structural Specialisation

Core paper · 72 marks with Engineering Mathematics

9 Units
  • Force–deformation and force–equilibrium relationships, generalised Hooke's law
  • Strain energy, Castigliano's theorem, statically determinate and indeterminate structures
  • Beams and frames, deflection of beams and frames, buckling of columns
  • Analysis of statically determinate and indeterminate structures, influence lines
  • Arches, three-hinged arches, cable structures, influence lines for indeterminate structures
  • Matrix methods of structural analysis, structural dynamics, stability of structures
  • Index and engineering properties of soils, permeability, seepage, effective stress
  • Compaction, consolidation, shear strength, earth pressure theories, slope stability
  • Bearing capacity, shallow and deep foundations, pile foundations, soil exploration
  • Working stress and ultimate strength methods, limit state design of beams, columns and slabs
  • Design of steel members: tension, compression, beams, columns, roof trusses, plate girders
  • Limit state design of concrete, durability, RCC detailing
  • Properties of fluids, fluid statics, buoyancy and floatation, fluid dynamics, Bernoulli's equation
  • Flow through pipes, minor and major losses, laminar and turbulent flow, boundary layer theory
  • Open channel flow, uniformly and non-uniformly varied flow, hydraulic jumps, energy and momentum equations, dimensional analysis and similitude
  • Hydrology: rainfall, evapotranspiration, infiltration, run-off, hydrographs, flood routing
  • Irrigation: water requirement, canal design, dams, spillways, irrigation structures
  • Water supply: treatment, distribution, sewerage and treatment, air and noise pollution, solid waste management
  • Highway engineering: geometric design, pavements, materials, construction and maintenance
  • Traffic engineering: studies, capacity and LOS, signal design, parking
  • Railway, airport and harbour planning, bridges and culverts
  • Building materials: cement, aggregates, bricks, timber, steel, composites
  • Building components, finishes, building services, estimating and costing
  • Construction planning, scheduling (CPM and PERT), project management, safety and quality control
  • Chain, traversing, compass, plane table, theodolite, tacheometry, triangulation and curves, remote sensing and GPS
  • Engineering geology: rocks, minerals, faults, folds, groundwater, geological hazards
